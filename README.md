# Marketing Campaign Performance & Sales Revenue Analysis

## Overview
This project analyzes 18,000 marketing campaign records to explore sales revenue, advertising efficiency, customer segments, and campaign performance. Python was used for data cleaning and exploratory data analysis (EDA), while Power BI was used to create an interactive dashboard.

## Tools & Technologies
- **Python:** Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn
- **Power BI:** Interactive visualizations and DAX measures
- **Jupyter Notebook:** Data cleaning, EDA, and business insights

## Key Findings
- **Marketing Channels:** Social Media generated the highest total revenue, while Influencer campaigns had the highest average revenue per record.
- **Marketing Efficiency:** Social Media achieved the highest overall revenue-to-ad-spend ratio (3.70).
- **Product Categories:** Stationery recorded the highest average sales revenue.
- **Seasonality:** Campaign performance showed seasonal variation, with stronger results around October and November.
- **Discounts:** Higher discount rates were associated with lower average sales revenue.
- **Competition:** High-competition campaigns generated approximately 11.9% lower average revenue than low-competition campaigns.

## Power BI Dashboard
The dashboard presents marketing KPIs, revenue trends, channel and customer segment performance, advertising efficiency, and the relationships between campaign characteristics and sales revenue.

## Conclusion
The analysis highlights how marketing channel selection, seasonality, discounts, and competition are associated with sales performance. These insights can support more informed marketing decisions and campaign evaluation.
